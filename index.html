On the road

Real reefers, real routes.

The problem

Perishables don't forgive a warm truck.

Anyone shipping cakes, dairy, seafood or frozen food knows the same three headaches. ColdRoute is built around fixing each one.

Finding a reefer you can trust

Dry-truck apps can't carry perishables. Here every vehicle is verified: registration, permit, insurance, licence, and hygiene and pest-control certificates. If a hygiene certificate lapses, the truck is blocked from cold jobs automatically.

Proving it stayed cold

When cargo arrives warm, receivers can claim damage or charge cooling fees. Continuous temperature logs, door-open events and the temperature at handover become the evidence that protects both shipper and carrier.

Mixing loads safely

Fish shouldn't share air with ice cream. Veg and non-veg stay apart. Pharma is isolated. A rules engine checks every load, and operations can edit the rules without waiting for a software release.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Is this like Porter?

      Similar booking experience, different job. Porter moves ordinary goods, mostly inside a city. ColdRoute is for temperature-controlled goods over longer distances, with temperature monitoring and proof built in.

      Can I ship medicines?

      Pharma is planned but gated. It opens only after licensing, packaging and quality validation are in place, and it always travels in its own compartment or vehicle.

      What if cargo arrives warm?

      The platform records continuous temperature, door openings and the handover reading. That record is the evidence for resolving a dispute fairly. Thresholds and compensation rules per cargo type are still to be agreed.

      Which cities will launch first?

      Pilot corridors are still to be decided. We expand one corridor at a time as the data supports it.

      I own reefer trucks. Can I join?

      Yes: as an owner-driver, a fleet owner or an aggregator. Join the early-access list and tell us your vehicle count and routes.
